# Go-Slow privacy notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Go-Slow is a story game that runs in your web browser. It is made by Chiamaka Nwabulue.

## What the game stores
- **The name you type** when you create your character (up to 16 characters; it does not have to be your real name).
- **The look you pick** (one of three pictures that come with the game).
- **Your saved game:** where you are in the story and the choices you have made.

These are kept only in your own browser's storage on your own device, so that "Continue" works. They are never sent to the game's maker or to anyone else.

## What the game does not do
- No accounts, sign-ups, emails or passwords.
- No photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location.
- No payments.
- No ads, analytics, tracking, social media plug-ins or outside fonts.
- No cookies.

## Deleting your data
Open the game, go to **Settings → Reset saved progress**. You can also clear this website's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app from your home screen.

## Hosting
The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) when the page is loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement at docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies.

## Age
The game is recommended for players aged 13 and over. It does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

## Contact
Questions about this notice: open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
