📖 How to Play

Go-Slow is an interactive story. You read, you choose, and your choices shape a whole month — and how it ends.

Controls

👆
Tap anywhere to advance dialogue. Tap once while text is typing to reveal it instantly.
🔀
Tap a choice when replies appear. Some choices change the story for good.
☰
Open the in-game menu to save & quit — progress also saves automatically.

Your stats (top bar)

⭐
Standing — how HR & your oga see you. Too low and you can be fired.
🔎
Clues — evidence you gather for the mystery.
₦
Money — survive the month and make rent.
❤️
Bonds — your relationships. Get close and people stand by you.

Tips

Watch for the reward badges that pop up — they mean a choice mattered. Be kind to people; it comes back around. And there is more than one way the month can end. 🚌

ℹ️ About

GO-SLOW

An interactive story set in Lagos, Nigeria. Survive a working month, chase a corruption scandal to the very top, fall in love, and decide how far you will go for the truth.

A month-long branching story · 11 characters · many endings.
Version 1.0.1

🔒 Privacy. Go-Slow has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no cookies. Your name, your look and your saved game stay on this device only, in the browser's storage. Nothing is sent to anyone. To delete it, use Settings → Reset saved progress, or clear this site's data in your browser.

⚠️ Content note. Recommended for ages 13 and over. The story includes workplace pressure, bribery and corruption, threats, and a scene where a character is attacked off-screen. Romance is between adults and stays tasteful.
