Survive a working month in Lagos — beat the go-slow, dodge the HR query, chase romance, and uncover a quiet office scandal. An interactive story wrapped around a daily-life sim.

2 · The Game Loop — one day, five phases

A run is one working month. Each day flows through five phases; survive to payday with your job and rent intact.

🌅MorningCheck weather, fuel & NEPA.
When do you leave?
🛣️CommuteTrek or ride through the chaos.
How do you travel?
💼WorkFace the oga, dodge queries.
Hustle or coast?
🌆EveningLight or NEPA, cook, socialise.
Gen or darkness?
🌙NightPay bills, sleep or hustle.
Rest or grind?
🔁 …then it loops to the next morning. WIN: reach payday employed & rent paid. LOSE: 3 HR queries (sacked) or run broke (evicted).

4 · How It Plays — the interaction model

Scenes play out Episode-style: characters walk in and move, the speaker lights up, dialogue appears in a box you tap to advance, and choices branch the story.

You
(Okay… how do I play this?) ▸
Avatars move. Characters enter from the sides and take positions; the one talking brightens, the rest dim.
Tap to advance. Dialogue appears with the speaker's name, colour and portrait — tap anywhere to continue.
Choices branch. At key moments you pick a reply; it changes the dialogue and your stats.

6 · What Your Choices Move

Five values react to everything you do — three from the story, two from the life-sim.

⭐StandingYour favour with HR & the oga. Too low → queries → the sack.
❤️BondCloseness with friends & love interests. Unlocks help and story.
🔎CluesMystery progress. Collected through trust and being in the right place.
💰MoneySalary vs the daily bleed of transport, fuel, food, rent.
⚡EnergySpent commuting & hustling, restored by sleep and good food.

7 · World Systems

The Lagos pressures your choices push against.

🏠 Ground floor
Cheapest rent — but floods in the rains.
🏢 Middle floor
Moderate rent — dry & safe. The smart pick.
🏙️ Top floor
Priciest, status — but the roof leaks.
🛣️ CommuteTrek, danfo, keke, okada, Bolt or your own car — cost vs speed vs risk.
💡 NEPA & fuelLight isn't guaranteed; run the gen (spend) or sit in darkness.
🌧️ WeatherRainy season spikes floods & go-slow; dry season brings heat & dust.
💸 EconomySalary once a month; bills bleed daily; rent is a full year up front.
